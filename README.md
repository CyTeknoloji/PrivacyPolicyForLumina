Privacy Policy for Lumina
Effective Date: March 2, 2026

Lumina ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, retain, and delete information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect
To provide you with a personalized experience, we may collect the following types of information:

Account Information: If you sign up using Firebase Authentication (e.g., Email or Google Sign-In), we store your User ID and email address to sync your data across devices.

App Data: We store your focus areas, daily ritual completions, favorite affirmations, journal entries, and related progress in Google Firestore to ensure your data is saved when you are signed in.

Device Information: We may collect basic information about your device (model, OS version) to improve app performance and fix bugs.

2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information to:

Personalize your daily rituals and affirmations based on your "Focus Area."

Save your progress (daily records) so you don't lose your data.

Improve app functionality and user experience.

Provide secure access to your account via Firebase.

3. Data Storage and Security
We use Google Firebase to store and secure your data. Your personal information is stored on secure servers, and we implement industry-standard security measures to protect it. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

4. Data Retention
We retain different types of data for different periods, as described below:

Account and Cloud Data (Firebase Firestore and Authentication): We retain your account information and synced app data for as long as your account remains active. This includes your profile, focus areas, ritual progress, daily records, favorite affirmations, and journal entries. When you delete your account (through the app settings or by contacting us), all associated cloud data is permanently deleted from our Firebase servers within 48 hours.

Local Device Data (SharedPreferences and Room Database): Data stored on your device (including guest-mode progress, notification preferences, and offline content) remains on your device until you uninstall the app, clear the app's storage/data in your device settings, or delete your account. Guest-mode data is not uploaded to our servers unless you create an account and sign in.

Daily Mood Check-In: Your selected mood for the day is stored locally on your device only and is automatically cleared when a new calendar day begins. We do not upload daily mood selections to our servers.

Analytics and Crash Reports (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics): Diagnostic and usage data is retained according to Google Firebase's default retention policies (typically up to 14 months for analytics). This data is used to improve stability and does not include the text of your journal entries or other personal wellness content.

Inactive Accounts: We do not automatically delete inactive accounts. Your data remains stored until you request account deletion.

5. Third-Party Services
Lumina uses the following third-party services:

Google Firebase: For authentication, database management (Firestore), analytics, and crash reporting.

Room Database: Local storage on your device for offline access to affirmations and journal entries.

6. Your Rights and Data Deletion
You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time.

In-App Deletion: Signed-in users can delete their account and all associated cloud data through the app settings.

Email Deletion Request: You may also send an email to cy.teknoloji@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion". Your request will be processed within 48 hours, and all your records (profile, rituals, focus areas, favorites, journal entries, and authentication data) will be permanently erased from our Firebase servers.

After account deletion, any remaining data on your device can be removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in your device settings.

7. Children's Privacy
Lumina does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the Effective Date above.
